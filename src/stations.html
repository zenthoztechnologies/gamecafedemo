Before you come in

Can I sign in to my own PlayStation or Steam account?

Yes. Sign in on any station and we wipe accounts automatically when your session ends. You can also use our accounts, which own everything in the library.

Do my saves carry over between visits?

On your own account, saves sync through the cloud as normal. On ours, ask the counter for a save slot under your name and we keep it for 90 days.

Is there an age limit?

Under-14s need an adult on site until 8pm and must leave by 8pm. The VR bay is 12 and over. We follow the age rating on every game.

Can I bring my own controller or keyboard?

Of course. Every PC has free USB ports at the front and the PS5s accept any DualSense or licensed fight stick.
