Questions about paying

When do I pay for a booking?

At the counter when you check in. Bookings are free to cancel up to two hours before your start time.

What happens if I play past my booked time?

If the station isn't booked after you, keep playing and we charge the extra time in 15-minute steps. We'll let you know ten minutes before anyone else needs it.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes, in any amount from $20 at the counter. They work for time, food and memberships.

Can I pause my membership?

You can pause once a year for up to two months at no charge. Ask at the counter or email us.
